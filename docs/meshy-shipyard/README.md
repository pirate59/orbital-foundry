# Shipyard Meshy integration

The imported gantry occupies two adjacent tiles in the same cell. The clicked tile remains the belt connection and building anchor; the second tile is reserved for the hull and gantry. During placement, Z rotates clockwise, Shift+Z rotates back, right-click rotates clockwise, and Esc cancels. The green or red preview shows both tiles.

The model is centered between the two tiles. Its long axis follows the selected orientation. Other building definitions and the 3×3 HQ rule are unchanged. Shipyards loaded from old saves automatically claim a clear adjacent tile; the first available direction is used. If all four neighboring tiles are occupied, the game shows an alert and claims one as soon as a neighboring belt or structure is cleared.

The cyan marker in `shipyard-marked.png` indicates the floating claw carriage. The red and yellow markers indicate the two moving side arms. Short orange sparks and blue-white weld arcs pulse along the work area. The screenshots show the claw at rest, partway down the ship, and after its return.

# Orbital Foundry milestone moments

Celebrate a *change in what the player can do*, not every routine cycle. Each moment below fires once per match for the local player; normal sales and machine cycles retain their quiet feedback. Keep celebrations brief enough that belts and building controls remain usable.

| Moment | Trigger | Suggested feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First refined bar | First Iron Bar made by the player's Refinery | Warm furnace flash, short two-note chime, small “First metal” toast. |
| First Signal Relay | First Relay made by the player's Assembler | Relay rises briefly above the machine, antenna pulse travels along its outgoing belt, brighter musical phrase. |
| First Relay sale | First Relay sold by the player's Sale Depot | Depot sign lights, credit total counts up, “Your first factory is trading” toast; complete the intro guide. |
| First research completed | First research level finishes | Branch node illuminates and sends a line toward newly available recipes; restrained discovery sound. |
| First drone delivery | A good crosses an asteroid gap and arrives through a Drone Pad for the first time | Brief flight trail and landing light; “Your network now reaches another asteroid” toast. |
| First craft launched | First player-owned craft leaves a Shipyard | Short launch camera emphasis, gantry lights and launch sound; preserve camera control and allow skipping. |
| First Fort deployed | Player's construction zone becomes a completed Fort in a claimed cell | Beacon sweep across that asteroid and a territory banner. |
| Trade goal reached | Player's sales rate first reaches the trade target | Top-bar target glows and hold timer becomes prominent; keep the signal calm because the rate may fall again. |
| Victory | Trade hold completes or the final opposing HQ falls | Full music resolution, faction-colour sweep and result screen. |

## Sequence and restraint

The first three moments form the opening arc: make a bar, make a Relay, sell a Relay. They should share a visual motif so the player understands that the factory has advanced. A repeat playthrough can use shorter versions. Never interrupt a battle, steal the camera during placement, or play several celebration sounds in the same instant; queue or combine simultaneous moments.

The Signal Relay is an early milestone, not a win condition. After its first sale, point the player toward Ion, research and expansion rather than another fixed tutorial chain.
